Marché couvert Beauvau + Marché d'Aligre

One of the most authentic market experiences in Paris, this is where the 12th arrondissement still feels entirely local. Outside, the open-air market spills into a lively patchwork of produce, preserves, and the occasional antique find; inside Beauvau, the mood shifts to finer cheeses, charcuterie, and beautifully run specialty stalls. It is wonderfully everyday, and all the better for not being staged for visitors.

Afternoon

Canal Saint-Martin stroll + Rue Beaurepaire

More local hangout than landmark, Canal Saint-Martin is where Paris softens into a slower rhythm. The tree-lined water, iron footbridges, and old lock system give the area its quiet character, while the quays attract the kind of easy afternoons that call for coffee, a stroll, and very little agenda. Along the canal, and especially on Rue Beaurepaire, there are plenty of independent shops and cafés with a distinctly understated, creative feel.

Late morning

Champ de Mars → Pont de Bir-Hakeim → Esplanade Joseph-Wresinski

This sequence brings together a few of the most photogenic Eiffel Tower viewpoints in Paris. Pont de Bir-Hakeim, known from Interstellar, has its striking double-level structure and cinematic lines, while Esplanade Joseph-Wresinski offers a broad, open angle with the full tower framed by lawn in the foreground. It is one of the most elegant ways to experience the classic Paris skyline without reducing it to a single stop.
